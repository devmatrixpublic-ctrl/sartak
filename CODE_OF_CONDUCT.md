# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project without harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps build a good community:

- Being kind and patient, and assuming good intent.
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the people Satark is meant to protect, who are often frightened or vulnerable.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address, phone number or payment detail, without their permission.
- Using this project's code or data to deceive, defraud or target real people.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behaviour they find inappropriate, threatening,
offensive or harmful. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when they do.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, through the repository's Security tab (**Report a vulnerability** works for conduct reports too) or by contacting a maintainer
through their GitHub profile and asking for a private channel. All reports are handled promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these steps, in order of seriousness:

1. **Correction.** A private, written note explaining what happened and why it was not appropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from the project's spaces for a serious breach.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban for a pattern of breaches, harassment, or aggression toward others.
